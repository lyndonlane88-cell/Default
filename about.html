About FastLane Courier

We're a St. Louis–based courier company built from the ground up to serve the industries that demand the most from their delivery partners.

Our Story

St. Louis–Built, Business-Focused

FastLane Courier Services LLC was founded with a simple belief: professional businesses deserve a courier service that operates at the same level they do. Not just someone who drops a package — but a trusted logistics partner who shows up on time, every time.

We started by serving the medical community — one of the most demanding environments for delivery. From there, we expanded into legal, manufacturing, and automotive, building a reputation as the courier that St. Louis businesses call when it matters most.

Today, FastLane operates across a 50-mile radius from St. Louis, delivering thousands of items per month for hospitals, law firms, factories, and dealerships throughout the region.
