Discount Programs

Ways to Save

Ask about these when you request your quote — discounted pricing isn't shown here because it's tailored to your account.

Founding Client Rate

Our first 10 clients lock in a discounted rate on their account. Let us know when you request a quote to see if a spot is still open.

Scheduled Daily Routes

Running the same route every business day? Recurring, scheduled routes qualify for discounted volume pricing — tell us your schedule and we'll build a rate around it.

Additional Fees

Good to Know

  • Special Packaging / Prep: $50, when an item requires special wrapping or packaging before pickup
  • Cancellation: 50% of the quoted price if canceled after a driver has been dispatched
  • Wait Time: first 15 minutes at pickup/drop-off free, then $15 per additional 15 minutes
Surcharges
  • +20% before 6:00 AM or after 8:00 PM weekdays
  • +20% after 5:00 PM Saturday
  • +30% Sunday and holidays
