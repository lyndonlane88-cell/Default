Privacy Policy

How FastLane Courier Services LLC collects, uses, and protects the information you share with us.

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how FastLane Courier Services LLC ("FastLane," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit fastlanecourierservices.com (the "Site") or submit information through our Contact, Get a Quote, or Driver Opportunities forms.

Information We Collect

When you submit a form on this Site, we may collect:

  • Contact details such as your name, email address, phone number, and company name
  • Delivery details such as pickup and drop-off addresses, service type, and scheduling preferences
  • Driver application details such as employment history and licensing information, if you apply through our Driver Opportunities page
  • Any additional information you choose to include in a message or notes field

We do not knowingly collect payment card information through this Site.

How We Use Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to quote requests, contact inquiries, and driver applications
  • Schedule and coordinate deliveries
  • Communicate with you about your request, account, or delivery status
  • Improve our services and this Site

Third-Party Services

Our Contact, Get a Quote, and Driver Opportunities forms are hosted through LeadConnector/GoHighLevel, a third-party form and customer-relationship platform. Information you submit through these forms is transmitted to and processed by that provider on our behalf, subject to their own privacy and security practices. This Site also loads fonts from Google Fonts and icons from a Font Awesome content delivery network (CDN); these providers may process limited technical data (such as your IP address) as part of delivering that content.

Cookies & Tracking

This Site itself does not set first-party tracking cookies. Third-party services embedded on this Site, including our form provider, may use cookies or similar technologies to operate their widgets and prevent form abuse. We do not currently run analytics or advertising tracking on this Site.

Data Retention

We retain information submitted through our forms for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, provide our services, and maintain business records, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

How We Protect Information

We rely on industry-standard safeguards from our hosting and form providers to protect information in transit and at rest. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights & Choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information you've submitted to us by contacting us using the details below. We will respond to reasonable requests consistent with applicable law.

Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
