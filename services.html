Medical Courier

When patient care is on the line, delays aren't an option. FastLane's medical courier service is built for the healthcare industry — HIPAA-aware, chain-of-custody compliant, and available around the clock.

We serve hospitals, outpatient clinics, diagnostic labs, pharmacies, blood banks, and specialty medical practices throughout the St. Louis metro area.

  • Lab specimens, blood samples, and biohazard materials
  • Medications and prescription transfers
  • Medical records and patient documents
  • Medical equipment and supplies
  • Organ and tissue transport coordination
  • STAT and routine delivery scheduling

Legal Courier

Deadlines in the legal world are absolute. FastLane delivers time-sensitive legal documents to courthouses, law firms, and clients with the accuracy and discretion the legal profession demands.

We understand filing deadlines, chain-of-custody requirements, and the need for a reliable proof-of-delivery — every time.

  • Court filings and clerk of court submissions
  • Subpoenas, summons, and process serving
  • Contracts, closing documents, and agreements
  • Deposition transcripts and exhibits
  • Confidential client correspondence
  • Title company and escrow document delivery
